          Good to know

          Common questions

          Do I need a permit for a fence?

          Usually not outside city limits. Inside city limits, fences over 8 feet generally need one. Rules differ by city, so check with yours if you’re unsure, and we’ll tell you what to expect when we quote.

          How close to the property line can the fence go?

          That depends on your lot and any easements. We work from your parcel information and measure from fixed points like the corner of the house. If the line is tight or disputed, a survey is the safest way to be sure.

          What fence types do you build?

          Cedar privacy (board-on-board, horizontal, shadowbox), split rail, galvanized and black vinyl-coated chain link, ornamental, and pedestrian and driveway gates.

          Can you repair my existing gate or fence?

          Yes. Post resets, hinge and latch replacement, re-squaring sagging gates, and storm damage repairs.
